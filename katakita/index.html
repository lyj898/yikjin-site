Why this works

Functional vocabulary first

Every word here was chosen because it gets used — ordering food, asking a price, finding a toilet — not because it fits a textbook chapter. Useful words stick because you reach for them.

Active recall

Reading a translation is recognition; producing it from memory is recall, and recall is what actually strengthens a memory. Each session moves from a quick look to genuinely being tested on it.

Spaced repetition

A word or two from earlier in the session quietly returns later on, after a short delay rather than right away — a simple, well-studied way to make a word more durable than drilling it once and moving on.

Hearing it spoken

Every word can be spoken aloud using your browser's built-in voice, so you're not just learning how a word looks — you're learning how it sounds, which is what you'll actually need in conversation.

Kata Kita is a small practice tool, not a certified course or a substitute for a class or a native speaker. Pronunciation is approximate — browser voices for Indonesian vary, and some devices may not offer one at all.
